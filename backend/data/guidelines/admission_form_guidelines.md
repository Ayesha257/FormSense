## 1. General Instructions

### 1.1 Writing the Form

Fill in the form in BLOCK LETTERS using a blue or black ball-point pen. Do not use pencil, gel colours or correction fluid. Write each answer inside its box; text written outside a box may not be read. Handwriting must be clear and legible: smudged, scribbled or overwritten entries that cannot be read are treated as not filled, and the form is returned to the applicant. All fields marked with * are compulsory.

### 1.2 Corrections

If you make a mistake, draw a single straight line through the wrong entry, write the correct value next to it and put your initials beside the change. Do not overwrite or scribble over a value. A struck-out and rewritten value will be checked by the Admissions Office, who may ask you to confirm it.

### 1.3 Values That Are Not Accepted

Do not write placeholders such as "N/A", "n/a", "nil", "None", "-", "XXXX" or a row of dots in a compulsory field. A placeholder is treated as a missing answer. If a field truly does not apply to you, contact the Admissions Office before submitting the form.

## 2. Section A: Personal Information

### 2.1 Full Name

Write your full name exactly as it appears on your CNIC, B-Form or Matric certificate. Do not use nicknames or initials only. The same name must be written again as the Declarant Name in Section D.

### 2.2 Father's Name

Write your father's full name as it appears on your CNIC or B-Form. If your father is deceased, still write his name; a guardian's name is not accepted in this field.

### 2.3 CNIC Number

Write your 13-digit CNIC number, or your B-Form number if you are under 18, in the format 12345-1234567-1: five digits, a dash, seven digits, a dash and one digit. Write digits only; do not use the letters O, l, S or B in place of 0, 1, 5 or 8, and do not leave out or add digits. The last digit of a Pakistani CNIC is odd for males and even for females, so it must agree with the gender you tick.

### 2.4 Date of Birth

Write your date of birth as it appears on your CNIC, B-Form or Matric certificate, in the format DD/MM/YYYY, for example 07/03/2006. It must be a real calendar date (there is no 31 April or 30 February). Applicants must be at least 16 years old on the date the form is signed.

### 2.5 Gender

Tick exactly one box: Male or Female. Leaving both boxes empty or ticking both makes the form incomplete. The gender ticked must agree with the last digit of the CNIC / B-Form number.

### 2.6 Email Address

Provide a personal email address that you check regularly, for example name.surname@gmail.com. All admission updates, test schedules and merit lists are sent to this address. A valid address has exactly one @ sign, a name before it and a domain such as gmail.com after it, with no spaces, commas or double dots.

### 2.7 Mobile Number

Write your own mobile number in the format 03XX-XXXXXXX: eleven digits starting with 03, with a dash after the first four digits. Landline numbers, numbers starting with 04 and numbers with missing or extra digits are not accepted. The number must be active, as interview calls and SMS alerts are sent to it.

### 2.8 Postal Address

Write your complete current postal address: house number, street, area and city. Incomplete addresses can delay the delivery of your admission letter.

## 3. Section B: Program Applied For

Tick ONE program only: BS Computer Science, BS Software Engineering or BS Artificial Intelligence. Forms with no program ticked, or with more than one program ticked, are incomplete and will not be processed. To apply for more than one program, submit a separate form and fee for each program.

## 4. Section C: Academic Record

### 4.1 Examination Years

Write the year in which you passed Matric / O Level and Intermediate / A Level as a four-digit number, for example 2023, exactly as printed on your result cards. The Intermediate year must be later than the Matric year, normally by two years.

### 4.2 Total and Obtained Marks

For both Matric and Intermediate, write the total marks and the marks you obtained in digits only, exactly as printed on your result card (for example 1100 and 893). Do not write marks in words. Obtained marks can never be greater than total marks.

### 4.3 Percentage

Write the percentage for each examination in digits. It must equal obtained marks divided by total marks multiplied by 100, rounded to two decimal places; for example 893 out of 1100 is 81.18. A percentage that does not match the marks will be queried.

## 5. Section D: Declaration

### 5.1 Declarant Name

Write your full name in the declaration exactly as written in the Full Name field of Section A. A different name in the declaration makes the declaration invalid.

### 5.2 Declaration Date

Write the date on which you sign the form in DD/MM/YYYY format. It must be a real calendar date, it cannot be in the future and it cannot be earlier than your date of birth.

### 5.3 Signature of Applicant

Sign the form yourself inside the signature box using a pen. A form without the applicant's signature is incomplete and is not accepted; a stray dot or mark does not count as a signature. Typed, printed or stamped signatures are not valid, and a parent or guardian may not sign on behalf of the applicant.

## 6. Submission

Submit the completed form with attested copies of your CNIC or B-Form, Matric and Intermediate result cards, two recent passport-size photographs and the fee deposit slip. Paste one photograph in the box on the first page. Forms can be submitted at the Admissions Office or through the online portal before the closing date.
